Reminders

Reminders always show inside the app. Notifications also alert you while the app is open in the background.

Quick add by voice

Brain Load can save a note from a link, so a voice assistant or shortcut can add to it for free. Everything after ?add= becomes a new note. Add &tag=work to file it under a tag.

iPhone, with Siri:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and make a new shortcut named “Brain Load”.
  2. Add Dictate Text, then URL Encode (on the dictated text).
  3. Add Open URLs and paste the quick-add link, then the encoded text after it.
  4. Say “Hey Siri, Brain Load”.

iPhone caveat: that link opens in Safari, which keeps its own copy of your entries, separate from the home-screen app. It works if you use Brain Load in Safari.

Android: share any text to Brain Load from another app, or press and hold the Brain Load icon and choose New note, then tap the microphone on your keyboard. Automation apps such as MacroDroid can open the quick-add link with text you dictate.

Install

On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop Chrome: use Install app in the browser menu.
